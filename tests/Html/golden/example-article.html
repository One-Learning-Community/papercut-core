Setting up two-factor sign-in

Updated 12 March 2026 · 4 min read

Before you start

Two-factor sign-in asks for a six-digit code from your phone after your password. Even if someone learns your password, they can't open your account without that code. You need:

  • An authenticator app on your phone, such as the one that comes with your password manager

  • About five minutes, and your phone within reach

  • Owner or Admin access, if you also want to require it for your whole workspace

Turn on two-factor sign-in

  1. Open Account settings and choose Security.

  2. Under Two-factor sign-in, select Set up and pick Authenticator app.

  3. Add Plotline to your authenticator app:

    • Scan the QR code on the screen, or

    • Choose "Enter a setup key" and type the key shown under the code.

  4. Type the six-digit code your app shows into the Verification code box and select Confirm.

  5. Save your recovery codes somewhere safe. You'll see them only once.

Note: Codes change every 30 seconds. If Plotline says a code is wrong, check that your phone's clock is set automatically.

Turn it on from the command line

If you manage accounts with the Plotline CLI, one command starts the same setup:

SHELL

plotline auth 2fa enable --method app
plotline auth 2fa status

The first command prints a setup key. The second shows enabled once you've confirmed a code.

Recovery codes

Each account gets ten single-use recovery codes. A recovery code works in place of the six-digit code, so keep them where you'd keep a spare key.

Treat recovery codes like passwords. Anyone who has one can sign in as you.

Require it for your workspace

Owners and Admins can make two-factor sign-in mandatory. Members who haven't set it up are asked to do so the next time they sign in, and they can't open any project until they have.

To turn it on, open Workspace settings, choose Security and switch on Require two-factor sign-in. You can set a grace period of 0, 7 or 30 days.

Guests are not affected, because they sign in with a one-time link rather than a password.
